Privacy Policy

Product: Digital Slate

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

Introduction

Digital Slate is a Chrome extension that provides a simple digital workspace for writing, drawing, rough work and managing multiple pages.

Information Collection

Digital Slate does not collect, sell, or transmit personally identifiable information to external servers.

Local Storage

Your saved slate pages, drawings and preferences are stored locally using Chrome's extension storage capabilities. This information remains within your browser and is not transmitted to our servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties. We do not use user data for advertising, profiling or tracking.

Permissions

Digital Slate uses only the browser permissions necessary to provide its writing, drawing and page management features.

Data Security

Since your slate data is stored locally in your browser, you remain in control of your saved content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the extension's functionality or privacy practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.
